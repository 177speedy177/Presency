STA 0+03 · Good to know

Questions, answered straight.

The stuff people actually ask before they hire a contractor.

Q1Do you handle the whole job, or just part of it?

The whole job. As your general contractor we plan it, pull what needs pulling, run the trades, and hand it back finished and cleaned up. One point of contact from the first walk-through to the last touch-up. full job scope

Q2How does pricing work?

We come look, we listen, and we give you a clear written price before anyone picks up a tool. If the scope changes mid-job, you approve the change and the cost before we move on it. No surprise line items at the end.

Q3Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry the licensing and insurance the work calls for, and we are glad to show proof before you sign anything. license # + insurance

Q4How soon can you start?

Depends on the size of the job and where the calendar sits, but we return every call the same day and get you on the schedule fast. Small repairs often happen within the week. typical start time

Q5Do you stand behind the work?

We do. If something we built is not right, we come back and make it right. warranty length + terms
