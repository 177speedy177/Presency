What we handle

Electrical services for your home

One licensed electrician for panels, wiring, lighting, EV chargers, and the repairs in between. All done to code. PLACEHOLDER: confirm & trim services

Panel Upgrades

Old fuse box or a panel that trips every time the AC kicks on? We swap it for a modern service that handles the whole house.

  • 100A to 200A service upgrades
  • Fuse box to breaker conversions
  • Sub-panels and circuit additions

Wiring & Rewiring

New circuits, more outlets, or a full rewire on an older home with cloth or aluminum wiring. Clean runs, labeled and terminated right.

  • Outlets, switches, and dedicated circuits
  • Whole-home rewires
  • Old and unsafe wiring replaced

Lighting Installation

Recessed cans, fixtures, under-cabinet, and outdoor lighting. Switched and dimmed the way you actually use the room.

  • Recessed and pendant lighting
  • Dimmers and smart switches
  • Exterior and security lighting

EV Charger Install

Charge at home overnight. We check your panel capacity, run the circuit, and mount your Level 2 charger to code.

  • Level 2 (240V) charger installs
  • Panel and load checks
  • Clean conduit runs

Troubleshooting & Repair

Dead outlets, breakers that trip, flickering lights, or that burning smell you cannot place. We track down the cause and fix it.

  • Breaker and outlet faults
  • Flickering and dimming lights
  • Code corrections and safety checks

Generator Install

Keep the fridge, heat, and lights on when the power goes out. Standby generators sized, wired, and tested for your home.

  • Standby generator installs
  • Transfer switch wiring
  • Sizing for your actual loads
